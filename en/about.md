      <p>
        I am <strong>Sunny (Coach Sunny)</strong>, a professional coach certified by the International Coaching Federation (ICF). For years, my work has focused on one simple thing: <em>helping people see their own inner mental habits, so they have the freedom to make better choices in life.</em>
      </p>
      <p>
        Each of us has an inner program running in our minds. It started when we were young. It helped protect us, but it also limited what we thought we could do. Often, our frustration does not mean we aren't good enough—it just means our old ways of thinking no longer fit the life we want to live today.
      </p>
      <p>
        In coaching, we look at this together: Where did this habit come from? What was it trying to protect? And how is it quietly shaping your choices and relationships?
      </p>
      <p>
        This blog is where I write down what I see. No hard theories, no quick tricks—just honest thoughts, real stories, and gentle questions. If something here helps you pause and understand yourself a little better, that is more than enough.
      </p>
      <p>
        May this space be like a warm cup of tea, keeping you company whenever you need a quiet moment. ☕
      </p>
